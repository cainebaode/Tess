tess使用帮助

Apple Silicon · macOS 26 或更新版本

开始听写

首次启动按引导允许麦克风和辅助功能，确认基础语音模型就绪并试说。把光标放到输入框，按住 Fn 说话、松开结束；也可以轻点 Fn 开始，再点结束。Esc 取消。若 Fn 同时打开表情或系统听写，可在系统键盘设置中选择“不执行操作”，两个自定义快捷键可在 Tess 中设置。

若提示“已复制”，回到目标输入框按 ⌘V。终端、检测到的密码框和安全输入状态不会自动输入。Tess 不会自动发送消息。

微信使用当前窗口的兼容输入，无法检查框内的光标、选区或输入框类型。请先点聊天输入框再录音，不要用于密码输入。中途点击浮窗以外位置、打字或切换应用后，结果改为复制。

两个自定义快捷键与提示词

Fn 默认用于本地基础听写；只有单独开启上下文校正后才调用文字服务。在“设置 → 通用与快捷键”中，为自定义快捷键 1 和 2 设置按键、选择绑定提示词；默认组合分别为 ⇧Fn 和 ⌥Fn。两个快捷键可以绑定同一条提示词，也可以分别用于润色和进一步加工。

在“设置 → 通用与快捷键”打开提示词库，也可从“文字模型”进入。默认提供轻量整理、自然表达、AI 任务指令、AI 简洁指令、英文翻译、条理清单、要点摘要和消息润色；先看用途与示例，再编辑或复制。点击键位可修改快捷键，旁边的省略号菜单可恢复默认或清除。先配置并测试文字服务，再使用自定义快捷键；语音仍在本机识别，只有转写文字发给所选服务。提示词可以缩短、重组或转换表达，原文始终保留。Fn 不受提示词选择影响。

常用词与上下文校正

在“设置 → 语音识别”添加标准写法、释义和常见误写，最多保存 50 项。词库默认关闭，只影响启用后的录音；Qwen 使用标准词与场景提示，Whisper 暂不使用词库提示。误写是候选，不是全局替换规则。词汇提示可能改善术语，也可能影响其他字词，请按真实录音效果决定是否启用。

可为 Fn 和两个自定义入口分别启用上下文校正。开启后，完整转写、相关词条及场景发送给你配置的文字服务；校正与模板使用一次请求。等待超过 8 秒或检查失败时使用原文，也可在浮窗点“使用原文”。模糊校正保留为候选，在结果面板确认、查看修改记录或恢复原文；已有输入请手动替换。修改结果后也可主动“记为常用词”。

从我的纠正中学习

在“设置 → 语音识别”主动开启，默认关闭，与手动常用词独立。编辑纯听写结果，或点击“提交改好的文字”主动粘贴对应整段。局部差异只产生候选；只有你确认是听错并点击“记住”，才保存为本地记忆。润色、翻译及文字模型结果不用于学习。

可读取的普通输入框仅在验证实际插入后短期观察，最长 120 秒；失焦、切换应用、下一次录音或关闭学习即停止。微信等无法可靠读取的场景使用主动提交，不模拟全选复制，不轮询剪贴板。

记忆默认只用于原应用；未知应用需明确选择所有应用。Qwen 参考正确写法，不做全局替换；Whisper 暂不使用个人学习提示。最多 50 条，支持停用、调整范围、删除、撤销和清空，冲突项暂停提示。关闭立即停止采集，下一次录音停用提示；清空也移除学习恢复副本。学习内容不自动加入远端文字校正材料，不保存日常录音、不训练模型。

浮窗默认跟随最新转录。向上滚动会暂停跟随，点击“回到最新”恢复；录音草稿仍可能修订，停止后用完整录音生成最终转写。

权限和恢复

麦克风没有声音：检查系统设置中的麦克风权限，并在 Tess 的“设置 → 语音识别”选择正确的麦克风。快捷键或自动输入失效：检查辅助功能权限，返回 Tess；若权限刚变更仍未生效，退出并重新打开。

模型下载失败：检查网络后重试。完整校验的文件会保留，未完成文件重新下载。基础听写不用配置文字 API；可选的文字整理需要你自己的服务配置。

更新

获取提供给你的新版 Tess DMG。退出 Tess，把新版拖入“应用程序”，选择替换，再从“应用程序”启动。模型和设置保存在独立的数据目录，不随应用替换而删除。当前版本采用手动更新，不会自动检查或下载新版。

隐私与清理

语音在本机处理。主动使用文字整理时发送待整理文字；启用上下文校正后还会发送转写、相关词条与场景。截图不发送给文字服务。常用词与各入口开关保存在本机偏好中，可随时修改、停用或删除。API 密钥存入 macOS 钥匙串。最近结果和截图草稿最长保留 24 小时，可在“设置 → 权限与数据”清除；额外下载的模型可在“语音识别”中删除，基础模型随应用一起移除，API 配置可在“文字模型”中删除。

本地数据位置：~/Library/Application Support/Tess。卸载应用不会自动删除这部分数据。Tess 不建立账号，不提供云中转或远程使用统计，不在后台读取剪贴板。

Help in English

Allow Microphone and Accessibility during setup, confirm the included speech model is ready, then try a sentence. Hold Fn to speak and release to finish, or tap Fn to start and tap again to stop. Escape cancels. If Fn also opens emoji or system dictation, change its action in Keyboard settings Custom shortcuts can be configured in Tess.

Place your cursor in a text field before recording. If Tess says Copied, return to your field and press ⌘V. Tess never sends messages. Terminals, detected secure fields and secure-input sessions use clipboard delivery.

WeChat uses window-level compatibility: Tess cannot inspect its cursor, selection or field type. Focus the chat composer before recording; do not use it in password fields. Clicking outside the overlay, typing or switching applications during recording changes delivery to copy-only.

Fn provides local dictation by default; it calls your text service only if you separately enable context correction. In General & shortcuts, customize two additional shortcuts and bind each to a prompt. Defaults are Shift+Fn for light cleanup and Option+Fn for agent tasks. Both may share a prompt. Open the prompt library from General & shortcuts or Text models to compare examples and edit or duplicate templates. Eight templates cover cleanup, natural wording, structured and brief agent requests, English translation, lists, summaries and messages. Click a shortcut to edit it; use its options menu to restore or clear it. Configure and test a text service first. Processing follows the prompt and may summarize or reorganize text. The original is retained.

In Speech recognition settings, add up to 50 personal words with meanings and mistaken spellings. Vocabulary hints are off by default and apply to Qwen, not Whisper. They can affect other words too. Enable context correction separately for Fn or either custom shortcut: the transcript, relevant words and scene are sent to your configured text provider in one request with your template. After 8 seconds or a validation failure, Tess uses the original; you can also choose Use original immediately. Review suggestions and changes in the result panel, restore the original, or explicitly remember a word. Copy again after editing an already delivered result.

Learn from my corrections is off by default and independent of manual vocabulary. Edit plain dictation, or submit its corrected passage. Only explicitly confirmed recognition mistakes become local memories. Text-service output is excluded. A readable field is observed only after verified insertion, for at most 120 seconds while focused. Unreadable fields, including WeChat, use manual submission without clipboard polling or simulated select-all/copy.

Memories apply to the original app; an unknown app requires an explicit all-app choice. Qwen receives correct spellings as hints, never replacement rules. Whisper does not use learning hints yet. Manage up to 50 memories: disable, change scope, delete, undo or clear. Conflicts pause hints. Turning learning off stops collection immediately and removes hints from the next recording. Clear also removes learning recovery copies. Memories are not automatically sent to text services; daily audio is not saved and models are not trained.

The overlay follows the latest draft until you scroll up. Choose Latest to resume. Drafts can change; final recognition uses the full recording.

For microphone issues, check permission and the input device in Speech recognition settings. For shortcut or insertion issues, check Accessibility and reopen Tess if needed. Download retries keep verified files. Basic dictation needs no API key.

To update, quit Tess and replace the app in Applications using the newer DMG supplied to you. Models and settings are preserved. This version uses manual updates and does not check for updates automatically.

Audio stays on your Mac. Optional editing sends text to your configured provider. Enabled context correction also sends the transcript, relevant vocabulary and scene. Personal vocabulary and correction switches stay in local preferences and can be changed or removed. API credentials stay in Keychain. Temporary text and screenshot drafts expire after 24 hours and can be cleared in Settings. Downloaded models and API configurations can be removed separately. The included base model is removed with the app. Uninstalling the app leaves its data in ~/Library/Application Support/Tess.
