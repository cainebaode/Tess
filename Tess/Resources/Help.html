tess使用帮助

Apple Silicon · macOS 26 或更新版本

开始听写

首次启动按引导允许麦克风和辅助功能，确认基础语音模型就绪并试说。把光标放到输入框，按住 Fn 说话、松开结束；也可以轻点 Fn 开始，再点结束。Esc 取消。若 Fn 同时打开表情或系统听写，可在系统键盘设置中选择“不执行操作”，两个自定义快捷键可在 Tess 中设置。

若提示“已复制”，回到目标输入框按 ⌘V。终端、检测到的密码框和安全输入状态不会自动输入。Tess 不会自动发送消息。

微信使用当前窗口的兼容输入，无法检查框内的光标、选区或输入框类型。请先点聊天输入框再录音，不要用于密码输入。中途点击、打字或切换应用后，结果改为复制。

两个自定义快捷键与提示词

Fn 固定用于基础语音转文字，不调用文字服务。在“设置 → 通用与快捷键”中，为自定义快捷键 1 和 2 设置按键、选择绑定提示词；默认组合分别为 ⇧Fn 和 ⌥Fn。两个快捷键可以绑定同一条提示词，也可以分别用于润色和进一步加工。

在“设置 → 通用与快捷键”打开提示词库，也可从“文字模型”进入。默认提供轻量整理、自然表达、AI 任务指令、AI 简洁指令、英文翻译、条理清单、要点摘要和消息润色；先看用途与示例，再编辑或复制。点击键位可修改快捷键，旁边的省略号菜单可恢复默认或清除。先配置并测试文字服务，再使用自定义快捷键；语音仍在本机识别，只有转写文字发给所选服务。提示词可以缩短、重组或转换表达，原文始终保留。Fn 不受提示词选择影响。

权限和恢复

麦克风没有声音：检查系统设置中的麦克风权限，并在 Tess 的“设置 → 语音识别”选择正确的麦克风。快捷键或自动输入失效：检查辅助功能权限，返回 Tess；若权限刚变更仍未生效，退出并重新打开。

模型下载失败：检查网络后重试。完整校验的文件会保留，未完成文件重新下载。基础听写不用配置文字 API；可选的文字整理需要你自己的服务配置。

更新

获取提供给你的新版 Tess DMG。退出 Tess，把新版拖入“应用程序”，选择替换，再从“应用程序”启动。模型和设置保存在独立的数据目录，不随应用替换而删除。当前版本采用手动更新，不会自动检查或下载新版。

隐私与清理

语音在本机处理。只有主动使用文字整理时，待整理文字才发往你配置的服务；截图不发送给文字服务。API 密钥存入 macOS 钥匙串。最近结果和截图草稿最长保留 24 小时，可在“设置 → 权限与数据”清除；额外下载的模型可在“语音识别”中删除，基础模型随应用一起移除，API 配置可在“文字模型”中删除。

本地数据位置：~/Library/Application Support/Tess。卸载应用不会自动删除这部分数据。Tess 不建立账号，不提供云中转或远程使用统计，不在后台读取剪贴板。

Help in English

Allow Microphone and Accessibility during setup, confirm the included speech model is ready, then try a sentence. Hold Fn to speak and release to finish, or tap Fn to start and tap again to stop. Escape cancels. If Fn also opens emoji or system dictation, change its action in Keyboard settings Custom shortcuts can be configured in Tess.

Place your cursor in a text field before recording. If Tess says Copied, return to your field and press ⌘V. Tess never sends messages. Terminals, detected secure fields and secure-input sessions use clipboard delivery.

WeChat uses window-level compatibility: Tess cannot inspect its cursor, selection or field type. Focus the chat composer before recording; do not use it in password fields. Clicking, typing or switching applications during recording changes delivery to copy-only.

Fn always provides plain dictation. In General & shortcuts, customize two additional shortcuts and bind each to a prompt. Defaults are Shift+Fn for light cleanup and Option+Fn for agent tasks. Both may share a prompt. Open the prompt library from General & shortcuts or Text models to compare examples and edit or duplicate templates. Eight templates cover cleanup, natural wording, structured and brief agent requests, English translation, lists, summaries and messages. Click a shortcut to edit it; use its options menu to restore or clear it. Configure and test a text service first. Processing follows the prompt and may summarize or reorganize text. The original is retained.

For microphone issues, check permission and the input device in Speech recognition settings. For shortcut or insertion issues, check Accessibility and reopen Tess if needed. Download retries keep verified files. Basic dictation needs no API key.

To update, quit Tess and replace the app in Applications using the newer DMG supplied to you. Models and settings are preserved. This version uses manual updates and does not check for updates automatically.

Audio stays on your Mac. Optional text editing sends text to your configured provider. API credentials stay in Keychain. Temporary text and screenshot drafts expire after 24 hours and can be cleared in Settings. Downloaded models and API configurations can be removed separately. The included base model is removed with the app. Uninstalling the app leaves its data in ~/Library/Application Support/Tess.
